A PRODUCT BUILT TO GROW

One case.
Independent layers.

The customer experience can grow without coupling bank authority to a language model. Transaction disputes are the first application of a general orchestration foundation.

Explore the system architecture
01 Savia · the customer and the case

Integrated voice, durable inquiries, completed perspectives, saved results, and the next visit. The recorded prototype demonstrates two reviewers.

02 FLUJO · the orchestration foundation

Generic chat, flows, tools, MCP, and execution interfaces. The extensible design connects ten teams of one lead and nine specialists, with separate root supervision. The exact 100-agent customer collaboration remains a qualification step.

03 Trusted host + Banking MCP · authority

Customer-owned facts, explicit consent, signed scoped calls, and receipt readback. The language model cannot grant itself banking permissions.

04 Data + evaluation · a traceable basis

Validated snapshots, row accounting, quarantine, lineage, fixed bilingual diagnostics, and an offline public fixture replay. Historical data defects informed the choice to ground inquiries in owned transactions.
