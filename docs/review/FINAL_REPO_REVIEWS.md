Each reviewer discovered substantial implemented successes: nineteen ordered R0–R18 rules, owned reads, clarification, explicit consent, verified simulated receipts and durable recovery. The core is a complete submission foundation independently of avatar or fleet extensions. Three reviewers freshly replayed all 1,078 core checks; the others ran focused existing suites. The reports retain the exact test denominators, network boundaries and incomplete attempts.

The useful follow-up findings concern exported Markdown links, preserving verified outcome caveats in voice, human label adjudication, and reconciling actual host outcomes in analytics. Review 4 independently demonstrated the voice presentation gap with a controlled provider mock: incorrect refund/resolution prose could reach playback history despite a trusted simulated-intake result. That demonstrates absent presentation enforcement; it does not demonstrate a live hallucination or a bank-action authorization bypass. The core's authority boundary held.

The final-video placeholder is an explicitly requested destination and was not treated as a missing feature. Larger fleet customer acceptance, human audio review, generic full-build reproduction and real-bank operation remain separately scoped. Reports found no blocker to presenting the declared fictional core and recorded examples as a working prototype.

The complete-submission rubric has five 20-point dimensions: technical judgment, AI engineering, data engineering, ML/evaluation and data analytics. Generic FLUJO uses architecture/extensibility 25, execution/integration 25, safety/recovery 20, reproducible engineering 20 and usability 10. These are review instruments, not official organizer numeric ratings.
