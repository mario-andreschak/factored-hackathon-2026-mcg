The new immutable build was complete and the previous build remained; this demonstrates publication/command-status ambiguity, not corrupt data or failed-contract publication. Write required external reports before the commit point, or explicitly report a successful publication with an optional report failure. Preserve blind worksheets, disagreements and separately versioned adjudicated labels. Because the diagnostic has already been inspected, future development also needs a new untouched set. The repository preserves three historical contributors; distinguish active delivery scope from historical credits rather than implying an independently measured staffing claim.

**Comparison quality**

The **routing comparison is the strongest controlled component comparison**: fixed C=8 and tau=.65, 120 frozen cases, 70/120 keyword versus 94/120 raw classifier, and 65/120 abstention. Reconstructing published decisions gives raw paired uplift of 20 percentage points, with a 10–30-point bootstrap interval. Human-required misses fall 13/30 → 3/30 → 1/30, while unnecessary human routes rise 6/90 → 9/90 → 52/90. Reporting both sides prevents claiming that lower misses alone justify deployment. AI labels, authored families and small denominators still constrain inference.

**Luna is transparent but measures a simpler boundary.** The 100 unique records, prompt/workload hashes, exact outputs, 50 ES/50 PT slices, turn overlap and baseline all reconstruct. Both provider and code achieve 100/100. Five scenarios with ten customer phrasings support exact grounding and concurrent transport, not generalized fluency, cooperating agents or an AI accuracy advantage. Its p50/p95 37.400/50.693 seconds include app-server admission/network/queueing.

**Infrastructure evidence is responsibly separated.** The first reference wording scored 217/300; a neutral wording repeat scored 300/300 through FLUJO, with queue-inclusive p50/p95 273.40/441.60 seconds. Eighteen live sandboxes, provisioning failures and operator steering are disclosed. Original private request histories were not available for this repository-only review; these committed historical aggregates do not establish a current 100-agent customer run.

The **ElevenLabs comparison is useful product framing but weak empirical evidence**. Vendor-reported inference time and Savia’s request-to-completion timings have different boundaries. A defensible next comparison would use identical ES/PT goals, allowed tools, ownership/action policy, case persistence, human escalation and latency/cost/quality measures. No superiority conclusion is warranted by the current commercial comparison.
