## Defects, gaps and comparison quality

No deterministic-core showstopper was demonstrated by the inspected implementation or successful checks. This does not certify every attack, semantic answer or platform failure mode.

The most concrete customer improvement is preserving protected qualifiers in speech. The native saved recommendation omits conditional folio advice and the explicit bank-not-resolved caveat still shown on screen. Exact trusted TTS or a constrained native result contract should retain those qualifiers for customers relying on audio.

The primary evaluation gap is independent human adjudication. The holdout provenance explicitly says it is required before final submission, while the proposed AI labels remain published and disclosed. The 78.3% versus 58.3% comparison is a useful fixed diagnostic, with paired uncertainty and a clear safety/escalation tradeoff; it cannot become human-verified or production-quality evidence. Abstention reduces human-required misses from 3/30 to 1/30 but raises unnecessary handoffs from 9/90 to 52/90. Reporting both is much better than promoting the lower miss count alone. The Luna workload transparently ties a deterministic baseline and uses ten repeated phrasings; it strengthens exact grounding/load evidence, without measuring superiority or collaboration.

Analytics currently lacks persisted token/cost observations and cannot join every trusted-host completion into a final workflow outcome. Its no-handoff containment numerator can include incomplete, blocked or unverified cases; it is not resolution. A final-outcome projection and cost/provider/source identity would make the proposed pilot measurable. Published operating analysis already avoids inventing ROI or pooling incompatible customer/app-server latency boundaries.

The ElevenLabs document is a good dated product/requirements comparison: direct vendor reference, declared vendor-reported claims, and no asserted superiority in quality, latency, languages, cost or compliance. Its explanation does not claim the vendor lacks workflows/tools/handoff. I did not fetch vendor content under repository-only instructions. The customer n=3 comparison crosses revisions and preserves a failed Portuguese answer plus a separate post-fix n=1; it is transparent but is not a controlled before/after study.

No broken local target was found among the 263 inspected links. Remote demo/film/vendor links were not checked. The portal's explicit final-video placeholder plus existing frozen film is the requested arrangement, so it is not a defect or completion blocker.
