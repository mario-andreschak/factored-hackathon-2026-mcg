# Channel clarifications on dataset quality

Reviewed 2026-09-30. This note records what the organizers and participants said in `#technical-help` and `#challenge-help` about issues relevant to our [full S3 review](DATA_REVIEW_2026-09-26.md). Channel replies are context, not a replacement for our measured [pipeline quality report](data-recovery/fresh_quality_report.md).

| Issue | Channel evidence | What we can conclude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transcript text and classifications | [Another team's transcript thread](https://factored-hackathon.slack.com/archives/C0BU1199KFX/p1790501174667709) reports scanning 171,321 transcripts, finding no dispute-specific dialogue, and seeing generic balance inquiries under `Queja` metadata. The organizer referred them to the documentation but did not identify another transcript table or explain the mismatch. | This independently supports our finding that the supplied transcript text and broad interaction tags are unsuitable as dispute-intent ground truth. It does **not** establish that no useful text exists in the other, unprofiled table families. |
| Historical complaint relationships | No reply in these two channels confirms a repair to complaint record links. Our [full S3 review](DATA_REVIEW_2026-09-26.md#critical-relationship-defects) found all 67,095 `origin_interaction_id` values empty, and all 44,570 populated `affected_product_id` values pointing to products owned by another customer. | Keep complaint records for aggregate demand estimates only. Do not join them to a customer-facing product, interaction, or transaction using those fields. |
| Dataset/document access | In an [S3 access thread](https://factored-hackathon.slack.com/archives/C0BU1199KFX/p1790367857859519), participants could not find connection information; an organizer said the document was updated. In a [separate document thread](https://factored-hackathon.slack.com/archives/C0BU54YAKMG/p1790372080058609), a participant said the Slack PDF lacked a page present in the emailed version. | These are document distribution/access reports. They do not show that any record-level relationship or label defect was corrected. Do not copy access details into this repository. |
| Transaction and processing dates | A participant raised `transaction_date > process_date`; the [organizer's replies](https://factored-hackathon.slack.com/archives/C0BU1199KFX/p1790410104886059) did not define either field or confirm a time-zone or orchestration explanation. Our [full event-date scan](data-recovery/event_date_alignment.json) found 3,318,701 same-day rows and 1,106,307 rows whose transaction calendar date is one day after `process_date`, with no other offsets. | Report the observed one-day pattern. Treat its cause and business meaning as unresolved; do not assert a time-zone or Airflow explanation. Keep the historical query window based on `process_date`. |
| Evaluation and supplementary data | The [organizer's evaluation reply](https://factored-hackathon.slack.com/archives/C0BU1199KFX/p1790363080139599) permits a prompted or fine-tuned LLM as the learned component if justified and rigorously evaluated against a reasonable baseline on the same held-out data, with valid labels and leakage prevention. An [external-data reply](https://factored-hackathon.slack.com/archives/C0BU1199KFX/p1790528058398079) permits justified external sources. | Use a clearly labeled, human-reviewed Spanish/Portuguese evaluation set and compare on a frozen holdout. The organizer did not explicitly approve synthetic dispute transcripts or certify the source labels in these replies. Our [pipeline report](data-recovery/fresh_quality_report.md#gold-outputs) reports that all 2,946 test rows have exact text also present in training, so the source split cannot support an unseen-text claim. |

## Team decisions

- Continue the owner-scoped transaction lookup; do not derive a historical complaint-to-transaction match from the broken links.
- Describe transcript/interaction categories as noisy source metadata and keep authored evaluation cases separate from organizer records.
- State date semantics as an open data-definition question. Cite measured offsets without claiming a cause.
- When discussing access fixes, say the documentation was updated; do not imply the underlying data was repaired.
