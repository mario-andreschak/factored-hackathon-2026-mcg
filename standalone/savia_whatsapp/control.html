Savia on WhatsApp

A separate local experiment with the hackathon RC. Savia answers text and voice notes using a fictional customer profile. It does not handle live WhatsApp calls.

Private operator access

Connect through your authenticated Fly tunnel, then enter this companion’s operator token. Reloading this page requires signing in again.

1. Set up your self chat

Enter your own account’s number. Scan the QR from that phone in WhatsApp → Linked Devices → Link a device. The number is your declaration: this MCP cannot verify the linked account’s own number.

Self-chat settings are saved locally. Restart the launcher to change them.

2. Test Savia

Start after pairing your own phone. Open “Message yourself” and send !savia Hola, ¿quién eres? or a voice note under 30 seconds. Savia processes only new notes after you start, then sends the exact text answer and a spoken reply. Its voice-note send does not prove you played it.
